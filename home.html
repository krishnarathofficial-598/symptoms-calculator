Welcome to kpTech Calc

Bridging the gap between sudden symptoms and accessible healthcare information. We built this platform to empower individuals to make informed decisions about their health quickly and securely.

Our Mission

Developed as a live symptoms calculator and disease predictor, this tool was engineered to provide instant, algorithmic analysis of common medical symptoms. Proudly built by a students of KCC Institute,("krishna prasad rath") and ("abhishek mishra") and ("vedant chauhan") this hackathon project demonstrates how clean code and logic can provide immediate value by suggesting over-the-counter remedies and identifying when it is time to consult a medical professional.
